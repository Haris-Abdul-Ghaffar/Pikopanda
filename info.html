    Good to know

    Sizing, shipping, returns and policies

    📏 Sizing

    Sizes can vary slightly between pieces and suppliers. Message us your child's age and height on WhatsApp and we'll recommend the best size before you order. When in doubt, we usually suggest sizing up for a longer fit.

    • Infants (0-24M): Measured by months. Allow room for growth.
    • Kids (2-12Y): Measured by age. Check height if your child is tall for their age.

    🚚 Shipping & delivery

    We deliver across Pakistan. Delivery charges and timing depend on your city and are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before your order is placed. Payment is Cash on Delivery, so you pay when your parcel arrives.

    • Major cities: 2-3 business days
    • Other cities: 3-5 business days

    🔄 Returns & exchanges

    If something isn't right, message us on WhatsApp as soon as you receive your parcel and we'll help sort it out. Please ask us for the exact return and exchange terms for your order before confirming it.

    • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and with original tags.
    • Requests must be made within 7 days of delivery.

    🔒 Privacy

    This website doesn't take payments, create accounts or collect personal details. When you tap an order button, a message opens in WhatsApp and is sent only if you choose to send it. Anything you share there is handled in WhatsApp and used only to process your order.

    📜 Terms

    This website is a catalogue. Browsing it or opening WhatsApp doesn't place an order: an order is only accepted when we confirm availability and price with you in chat. Prices are in PKR, delivery is charged separately, and colours may look slightly different on different screens.
