Questions, answered

Why is there no cart or checkout?

We keep ordering personal. You message us on WhatsApp, we confirm everything with you, and nothing is charged online.

How do I order?

Choose a size, tap “Order on WhatsApp”, and send the pre-filled message. We reply to confirm availability, price and delivery.

What is a SKU?

It's the product code shown on each card (for example PP-GRL-FLC-001). It helps us identify exactly the piece you want.

Do I pay in advance?

No. Orders are Cash on Delivery. You pay the courier when your parcel arrives.

How do I choose the right size?

Message us your child's age and height and we'll recommend a size. Sizes can vary slightly between pieces.

How much is delivery and how long does it take?

Delivery charges and timing depend on your city and are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before the order is placed.
